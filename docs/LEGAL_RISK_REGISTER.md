## Notes

- No direct evidence found of league or club logos, copyrighted photography, or explicit official partnership claims in active routed pages.
- Player Market safeguards implemented in product design: no cash deposits, no withdrawals, no paid purchase of market currency, no loot-box mechanics, and no odds/betting language.
- This register is not legal clearance; owner and legal verification is required before production commercialization.
- This register only includes issues supported by repository evidence at the time of review.
